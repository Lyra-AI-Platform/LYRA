Fuelwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Fuelwise is an iPhone app for comparing UK petrol and diesel prices. It is made by Lyra Labs, an independent UK developer, which is responsible for your information under UK data protection law.

Contact: legal@lyraauth.com

The short version

  • You can search by town or postcode, or let the app use your location to find nearby stations.
  • The app only uses your location while you are using it. It does not track you in the background.
  • Your favourite stations are saved on your iPhone.
  • Searches and maps are handled by outside services, which receive your search and your IP address.
  • The app shows adverts from Google AdMob. It asks for non-personalised ads, but these still involve some processing of information about your device.

Searching and your location

You can find stations by typing a town or postcode. You do not have to share your location.

If you choose to, you can let Fuelwise use your iPhone's location to find stations near you. iOS asks your permission first. Fuelwise uses your location only while the app is open and in use. It does not collect your location in the background. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Fuelwise.

Favourite stations

Stations you mark as favourites are stored locally on your iPhone. They are removed if you delete the app.

Outside services that handle your requests

To look up places and show maps, the app sends requests to these providers. They receive what is needed to answer the request, which can include the town or postcode you searched for, location-related requests (such as the area shown on the map) and your device's IP address. Each provider handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

Fuelwise is free and shows adverts provided by Google AdMob.

  • The app requests non-personalised ads. These are not based on your past behaviour, but Google can still use information about your device (such as device type, operating system, IP address and approximate location derived from it) to deliver the ad, limit how often you see it, prevent fraud and measure performance.
  • Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message before ads load, and you can make or change your choice there.
  • Sponsored offers: Fuelwise may also show offers directly from fuel retailers or other sponsors. These are matched only to the town or postcode area you searched and the fuel type you selected, not to you personally.

Your advertising choices:

  • In iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you stop apps asking to track you.
  • You can review your choices in the app's ad consent message, where shown.
  • Google explains how it uses data in apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

How long information is kept

Favourites stay on your iPhone until you remove them or delete the app.

Lyra Labs keeps no logs, analytics or search data from the app, because none of it reaches us. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as needed to answer it and for up to 12 months after.

The outside providers listed above keep information according to their own policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it
  • ask to receive information you gave us in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it, for example for location or ads

Because favourites are stored on your device, you can delete them yourself in the app or by deleting the app. For information held by the providers above, you can also contact them directly.

To use your rights, email legal@lyraauth.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Fuelwise uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
