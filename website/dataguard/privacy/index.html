1. Who we are

DataGuard is an iPhone app that helps you make and keep track of data protection requests to organisations, such as subject access and erasure requests under UK data protection law. It is made by Lyra Labs ("Lyra Labs", "we", "us"), which is responsible for your information under UK data protection law (the "controller"). Legal details are in the notice at the end of this page.

Contact: legal@lyraauth.com

2. The short version

  • You do not need an account with us, and we never ask you to sign up.
  • The details you put in your letters (such as your name and address) and your request history are stored on your iPhone, not on our systems.
  • DataGuard drafts letters on your iPhone. They are only sent when you send them yourself through Mail or another app you choose.
  • Deadline reminders are scheduled on your iPhone using iOS notifications.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. They are only personalised if you agree in Google's consent message and Apple's tracking prompt. Ad-free and Pro subscribers see no ads. There are no analytics.
  • Subscriptions are optional and are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information. Ads are only personalised using other companies' apps and websites if you allow it.

3. What this policy covers

This policy covers the DataGuard iPhone app. It does not cover the websites, apps or services of anyone else, including Apple, even where DataGuard links to them or uses their features. Those organisations are responsible for their own handling of your information. If you contact us about Lyra Labs' other products, the privacy policy at lyraauth.com/pages/privacy.html also applies.

5. Permissions the app asks for

Notifications

DataGuard asks permission before scheduling reminders for response deadlines. You can change this in Settings → Notifications → DataGuard.

6. Information stored on your iPhone

Everything you enter in DataGuard is stored locally on your iPhone in the app's own storage. We cannot see it and cannot recover it for you. It is removed if you delete the app, and may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own terms. You can delete individual items in the app, or everything from the app's Settings screen.

Adverts and subscriptions

The free version of DataGuard shows adverts from Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen adverts between activities (never more than one every few minutes), and an optional "watch an ad to try Pro for an hour" advert that only plays if you choose it. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. The app also asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Either way, Google uses information about your device to deliver ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

The optional Ad-free and Pro subscriptions are sold by Apple through your Apple ID. They renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple ID settings. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active; we do not receive your name or payment details.

8. Our legal reasons for using information

  • Consent: using permissions you grant through iOS. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Legitimate interests: providing the features you use, showing ads in the free version (personalised only with your consent) and answering your emails. We keep the information involved to a minimum.
  • Legal obligation and legal claims: keeping records the law requires and correspondence needed for legal claims.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Information sent outside the UK

Apple and Google may process information in the United States, the European Economic Area or elsewhere, using the safeguards available under UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

10. How long information is kept

  • Information in the app stays on your iPhone until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Lyra Labs keeps no analytics, usage history or location history from the app.
  • If you email us, we keep your message for as long as needed to answer it and for up to 12 months after, or longer if needed for a legal claim or a legal obligation.

11. Security

Your information stays on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection when your device is locked. Keep a passcode on your iPhone. No app can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee security. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires it.

12. Children

DataGuard is not designed for or directed at children. We do not knowingly handle personal information of anyone under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us and given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to be told how your information is used, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to limit or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. Because the app keeps your information on your iPhone, we usually hold nothing about you unless you have emailed us. To use your rights, email legal@lyraauth.com. We will reply within one month, which the law lets us extend by up to two further months for complex requests.

14. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or build profiles of you. Ads in the free version are personalised only if you agree.
  • We do not use your location in the background, and we only allow Google to personalise ads if you agree.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will update the date at the top, and if a change materially affects how your information is used we will tell you in the app or on the App Store listing before it takes effect.
