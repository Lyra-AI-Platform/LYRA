MacroAnvil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the MacroAnvil iPhone app handles, why, who else receives it, how long it is kept and what rights you have. Part B sets out the terms on which the app is provided. By downloading or using MacroAnvil you confirm that you have read this policy and agree to Part B.

1. Who we are

MacroAnvil is an iPhone app for logging workouts, food, supplements, water and body measurements. It is made by Lyra Labs ("Lyra Labs", "we", "us"), which is responsible for your information under UK data protection law (the "controller"). Legal details are in the notice at the end of this page.

Contact: legal@lyraauth.com

2. The short version

  • Everything you log (workouts, food, supplements, water, weight and body measurements, goals) is stored on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, and it is never stored in iCloud.
  • You do not need an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft, the sign-in details are kept in your iPhone's keychain. This version of the app does not upload your data to any server.
  • Apple Health, the camera, the microphone and Bluetooth are used only if you allow them, and only for the features described in section 5.
  • When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts and the US Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central to look up nutrition details. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • MacroAnvil Pro is optional and is sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • MacroAnvil gives general information to help you track your own habits. It is not medical advice.

4. Health information

Body measurements, nutrition and workouts are health information. MacroAnvil keeps it on your iPhone, does not use it for advertising or marketing, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone except Apple Health when you turn that on. Data read from Apple Health is used only to show your progress and adjust your targets inside the app.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Apple Health: to read weight, body fat, lean mass and activity, and to save workouts, food, water and measurements, if you allow it.
  • Camera: to scan barcodes and nutrition labels and to photograph meals.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to log food by voice.
  • Bluetooth: to read weight from a compatible smart scale.

You can change any of these at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

7. Our legal reasons for using information

  • Explicit consent for health information and for the permissions you grant in iOS. You can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Legitimate interests for providing the features you use and answering your emails.
  • Legal obligation and legal claims for records the law requires.

8. Information sent outside the UK

Food look-ups go to Open Food Facts (France) and USDA FoodData Central (United States). They contain only your search words or a barcode number. Apple, Google and Microsoft may process information in other countries using the safeguards available under UK law.

9. How long information is kept

Information in the app stays on your iPhone until you delete the entries or delete the app. We keep no analytics, usage history or logs from the app. If you email us, we keep your message for up to 12 months after we answer, or longer if a legal claim needs it.

10. Security

Your information is protected by iOS data protection when your iPhone is locked. Keep a passcode on your iPhone. No app can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee security.

11. Children

MacroAnvil is not designed for or directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly handle personal information of children.

12. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to be told how your information is used, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to limit or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw consent. Because MacroAnvil keeps your information on your iPhone, we usually hold nothing about you unless you have emailed us. You can export everything from the app's Settings, delete individual entries in the app, or delete the app to remove it all. To use your rights, email legal@lyraauth.com. We reply within one month.

13. Changes to this policy

If we add cloud backup or change how information is used, we will update this policy and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
