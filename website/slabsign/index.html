Questions

Common questions

Does my customer need to download anything?

No. They open the link on any phone, tablet or computer, zoom into the drawing, ask questions and sign with a finger or mouse. No app, account or password.

What exactly does the PDF record show?

The drawing with every numbered mark, the conditions they ticked, their typed name and drawn signature, the date and time, and a fingerprint of that exact version. If anything changes later, it’s a new version that needs a new signature.

Is a signature like this legally valid?

In the UK, electronic signatures are generally accepted as evidence of agreement. SlabSign keeps a clear record of what was approved, when and by whom. It doesn’t replace your terms and conditions, and for anything unusual you should check with your own adviser.

Does it work with my CAD or templating software?

Yes, if you can export a picture. A screenshot or image of the drawing, a scan of the template or a photo of the slab all work.

What if the customer wants changes?

They can pin a comment on the exact spot or ask for changes. You edit the layout and send it again, and the old link points them to the new version.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Pay as you go has no subscription, and monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Records you’ve already collected stay available to download.
