The expensive problem

Layouts get approved over scattered texts and screenshots. Then the slab is cut.

One disputed seam on a £6,000 quartzite island costs more than ten years of SlabSign.

SlabSign is not another shop-management system. It does one job: getting a clear, provable approval for exactly what you’re about to cut.

How it works

Four minutes from drawing to signature.

01

Upload

A CAD screenshot, the template drawing, or a photo of the actual slab.

02

Mark up

Seams, sink and hob cut-outs, vein direction, edge profiles, overhangs, notes. Each gets a number.

03

Send a link

By email, WhatsApp or text. The customer needs no app or account. They zoom, ask questions, pin comments.

04

Freeze & prove

They tick every condition and sign. The revision is locked with a fingerprint and a PDF record. Any edit becomes a new revision.
