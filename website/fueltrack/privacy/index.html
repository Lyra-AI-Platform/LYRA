1. Who we are

FuelTrack is an iPhone app for comparing UK petrol and diesel prices. It is made by Lyra Labs ("Lyra Labs", "we", "us"), which is responsible for your information under UK data protection law (the "controller"). Legal details are in the notice at the end of this page.

Contact: legal@lyraauth.com

2. The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • You can search by town or postcode, or let the app use your location to find nearby stations.
  • The app only uses your location while you are using it. It does not track you in the background.
  • Your favourite stations and settings are saved on your iPhone, not on our systems.
  • Searches and maps are handled by outside services, which receive your search and your IP address.
  • The app shows adverts from Google AdMob. It asks for non-personalised ads, but these still involve some processing of information about your device.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Fuel prices come from third parties and can be wrong or out of date. Always check the price at the pump.

3. What this policy covers

This policy covers the FuelTrack iPhone app and any web version or price service that Lyra Labs runs for it. It does not cover the websites, apps or services of anyone else, including the providers listed in section 7, fuel retailers, advertisers, Apple or Google, even where FuelTrack links to them or shows their content. Those organisations are responsible for their own handling of your information, and you should read their policies.

If you contact us about Lyra Labs' other products, the privacy policy at lyraauth.com/pages/privacy.html also applies.

5. Searching and your location

You can find stations by typing a town or postcode. You do not have to share your location.

If you choose to, you can let FuelTrack use your iPhone's location to find stations near you. iOS asks your permission first. FuelTrack asks for location only after you tap the option to use it, uses it only while the app is open and in use, and does not collect it in the background. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → FuelTrack.

When you search near your location, the coordinates (or the area around them) are sent to the place and station providers in section 7 so they can return nearby results. We do not receive or keep a history of your locations.

6. Information stored on your iPhone

Stations you mark as favourites, your chosen fuel type and similar settings are stored locally on your iPhone, using the app's own storage. We cannot see them. They are removed if you delete the app, and may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own terms.

8. Fuel price data and our price service

Fuel prices shown in FuelTrack come from retailers' published price lists and other third-party sources. To keep prices current, FuelTrack may download them from a price service run by Lyra Labs instead of from each retailer directly. That request does not include your search, location or any identifier for you. Like any internet request it carries your IP address and basic technical details, which our server may record in short-lived security logs used only to keep the service running, find faults and prevent abuse.

9. Advertising and consent

FuelTrack is free and shows adverts provided by Google AdMob.

  • The app requests non-personalised ads. These are not based on your past behaviour, but Google can still use information about your device (such as device type, operating system, IP address and approximate location derived from it) to deliver the ad, limit how often you see it, prevent fraud and measure performance.
  • Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message before any ads load. If you do not give consent, or consent cannot be confirmed, the app does not request ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy options the consent message provides.
  • FuelTrack does not ask for Apple's permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not do so.
  • Sponsored offers: FuelTrack may also show offers directly from fuel retailers or other sponsors. These are matched only to the town or postcode area you searched and the fuel type you selected, not to you personally, and no information about you is passed to the sponsor. Sponsored content is labelled.

Your advertising choices:

  • In iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you stop apps asking to track you.
  • You can review your choices in the app's ad consent message, where shown.
  • Google explains how it uses data in apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We are not responsible for the content of adverts or sponsored offers, or for any goods, services or websites they promote. Any dealings you have with an advertiser are between you and that advertiser.

10. Our legal reasons for using information

UK data protection law requires us to have a legal reason (a "lawful basis") for each use of personal information. Ours are:

  • Consent: using your device location (given through iOS) and, where required, advertising (given through Google's consent message). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Legitimate interests: sending the requests needed to run the features you use, keeping the app and any price service secure and working, preventing fraud and abuse, and showing non-personalised ads that keep the app free. We have weighed these interests against your rights and kept the information involved to a minimum.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records we are required to keep, and answering requests from authorities where the law requires it.
  • Legal claims: keeping correspondence where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

11. Information sent outside the UK

Some providers in section 7, including Google and Apple, may process information in the United States, the European Economic Area or elsewhere. Where personal information leaves the UK, it is protected by the safeguards those providers rely on under UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

12. How long information is kept

  • Favourites and settings stay on your iPhone until you remove them or delete the app.
  • Lyra Labs keeps no analytics, search history or location history from the app.
  • Security logs from any price service we run are kept for no more than 30 days, unless needed longer to investigate a specific security incident or abuse.
  • If you email us, we keep your message for as long as needed to answer it and for up to 12 months after, or longer if needed for a legal claim or a legal obligation.
  • The outside providers in section 7 keep information according to their own policies.

13. Security

FuelTrack connects to its providers over encrypted HTTPS connections, keeps your favourites on your device and does not ask for passwords or payment details. We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the small amount of information we handle. No app or internet service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the security of information sent over the internet. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires it.

14. Children

FuelTrack is meant for drivers and is not designed for or directed at children. We do not knowingly handle personal information of anyone under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us and given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

15. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal information is used (this policy)
  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it, including use based on legitimate interests
  • ask to receive information you gave us in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it, for example for location or ads
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you

Because we hold almost no information that identifies you, we may be unable to find anything about you unless you have emailed us. Favourites and settings are on your device, so you can delete them yourself in the app or by deleting the app. For information held by the providers in section 7, contact them directly.

To use your rights, email legal@lyraauth.com. We may need to ask for information to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month, which the law lets us extend by up to two further months for complex requests. Using your rights is free, but we may refuse or charge a reasonable fee for requests that are clearly unfounded or excessive, as the law allows.
