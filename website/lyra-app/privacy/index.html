Lyra for iPhone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the Lyra news app for iPhone and its Share extension. The lyraauth.com website has its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to read Lyra in the app.
  • Stories you save, your chosen countries and topics, and your settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Translation runs on your iPhone using Apple's Translation feature. Story text is not sent to us for translation.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, personalised only if you agree. Watch Pro subscribers see no ads.
  • We do not sell your information and the app has no third-party analytics.

When you open an original article, it loads from the publisher's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Adverts

The free version shows adverts from Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen adverts at natural breaks (never more than one every few minutes, and never when you first open the app), an advert when you come back to the app after a long time away (at most once every four hours), and an optional "watch an ad for an hour of Pro" advert that only plays if you choose it. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice later in the app's settings.

The app also asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Either way, Google receives information about your device (such as device type, iOS version, IP address and an approximate location derived from it) and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Subscriptions

Watch Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription sold by Apple through your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active; we never receive your name or payment details.

Your rights and contact

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the use of personal information we hold about you, to object to its use, and to complain. Because the app keeps your information on your iPhone, deleting the app deletes it. For questions or to use your rights, email legal@lyraauth.com. We aim to reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to deal with your concern first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly handle their personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be described in the app's update notes.
