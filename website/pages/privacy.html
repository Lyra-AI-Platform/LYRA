Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Lyra Labs · legal@lyraauth.com

What We Collect

When an end user completes a LyraAuth challenge, we collect:

  • The challenge response (text or selection)
  • Response timing (milliseconds)
  • A random session identifier (not linked to any personal identity)
  • Browser user agent string

We do not collect: names, email addresses, IP addresses, cookies, or any cross-site tracking data.

Lyra Accounts

The above covers people answering a LyraAuth challenge. If you create a Lyra account on lyraauth.com, we also hold:

  • Your email address and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself)
  • Your plan, trial dates and usage counts
  • Two sign-in cookies: lyra_session (keeps you signed in, unreadable by page scripts) and lyra_in (tells the page you are signed in). Both expire after 7 days or when you sign out. They are needed for the site to work and are not used for tracking.

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number; we keep only the Stripe customer and subscription IDs. Stripe's own policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Account data is kept while your account is open and deleted within 30 days of you asking us to close it, except billing records we must keep by law (normally 6 years).

How We Use It

  • Bot detection: Determine whether the respondent is human
  • AI training: Anonymized responses become training examples for Lyra AI models
  • Service improvement: Aggregate analytics to improve challenge quality

AI Training Disclosure

By answering a LyraAuth challenge, users consent to their anonymized response being used to train AI models operated by Lyra Labs. This disclosure is shown directly in the LyraAuth widget at the time of challenge completion.

Data Retention

  • Session tokens: deleted within 24 hours of verification
  • Anonymized training records: retained indefinitely (no personal data linkage)
  • User agent strings: retained for 30 days then deleted

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Since responses are anonymized and not linked to any identity, we cannot identify individual records. To exercise rights: legal@lyraauth.com

No Third-Party Tracking

We do not sell data to third parties. We do not use advertising networks. We do not fingerprint users across websites. This is the opposite of Google reCAPTCHA.

Who We Are
