Security Policy

How to report a security problem in Lyra Labs services

Reporting a problem

Email support@lyraauth.com with "Security" in the subject. Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what it could affect.

In scope

lyraauth.com, its APIs and the LyraAuth widget.

Testing rules

  • Use only your own accounts and data
  • Do not run denial-of-service tests, spam or social engineering
  • Stop and tell us if you reach someone else's data, and do not keep a copy
  • Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before publishing it
