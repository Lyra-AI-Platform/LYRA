## LinkedIn

I've launched Lyra Watch, a live tracker of the people moving the news.

It reads the coverage of politicians, investors, executives and public figures, finds everyone the media names, and gives each one:
• their job and a 0–100 popularity rating (Wikipedia readership + headline volume)
• documented party membership and office — sourced, never inferred
• the issues their coverage is about and the companies they're linked to
• their own posts (YouTube, Bluesky, official sites) and disclosed trades from SEC and House filings

Lyra Watch Live streams new stories and posts as they land, for anyone following markets, policy or media.

Every item links to its public source, and every person has a stable JSON link for AI tools.

Free directory: https://lyraauth.com/watch/
Live: https://lyraauth.com/watch/live/

## Hacker News (Show HN)

Title: Show HN: Lyra Watch – who's in the news, with sourced popularity, politics and posts

Text:
Lyra Watch tracks ~40 politicians, investors and executives and everyone the coverage of them names (~100 public figures at a time). For each person it shows a popularity score (70% 30-day Wikipedia pageviews on a log scale, 30% headline count), party and office from Wikidata (only documented facts — it never infers views), topic mix of their coverage, linked companies, their own posts where a free feed exists (YouTube/Bluesky/Mastodon/official RSS), and SEC Form 4 / 13F / House PTR trades.

Names are found by rules rather than an LLM: a given-name anchor plus a census surname check, sentence-case fallbacks, surname resolution and Wikipedia-title merging. It's cheap enough to run on a home NAS. Private individuals who appear in stories aren't published.

Everything has stable JSON URLs plus a JSON Feed and /llms.txt. The directory is free; the real-time stream is part of the paid plan.

https://lyraauth.com/watch/
